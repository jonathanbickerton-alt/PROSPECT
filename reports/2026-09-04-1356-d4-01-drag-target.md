## Item 1 — the engine

**`rebalanceToTarget`** in `src/utils/mixConstraint.ts`, a sibling of
`rebalance`. `rebalance` is **not widened** — its signature and its no-target
callers are untouched, and a check asserts that.

### The shape decided the design

With the moved member at `x`, the untouched unlocked members must satisfy

```
sum si      = R(x) = freeBudget - x                        (the sum)
sum si * ai = B(x) = 100*target - lockedArpu - x*aM        (the blend)
```

Both linear in `x`. For non-negative shares summing to `R`, the reachable blend
contribution is exactly `[R*minA, R*maxA]` — the same vertex argument
`achievableTargetRange` already makes. So **feasibility in `x` is an interval,
and the wall is its endpoint**: the clamp point is *derived*, not chosen, which
is what made decision 2 implementable rather than a matter of taste.

### One solve path, not two

The brief asked for an exact solve at two untouched members and minimum change
above that. They are the same computation: with two members the equations
determine the answer uniquely, and a least-squares solve returns exactly that
point. So there is one path, and no risk of two disagreeing at the boundary.
Minimum change is least squares under the two equality constraints (closed form
via Lagrange) with an **active-set loop** for non-negativity — pin whatever goes
negative, re-solve — which terminates in at most one pass per member and is
deterministic. Two runs of the same drag must agree or the control is not
predictable.

**Minimum change is not a recommendation.** Among reachable points it takes the
nearest one, which is the only rule that adds no opinion about which reachable
mix is good. Goal-seek-as-recommendation stays out of scope.

## Item 2 — both drag handlers

### Measured: the condition is shared, the fallback is not

The adapter **returns null when no target is applied** rather than falling back
to `rebalance` itself, because the two cards' no-target paths are genuinely
different: the Value card goes through `autoBalanceMix`, which carries a seeding
guard for a slider moved between a tier appearing and `seedMixPreserving`
writing it. Folding that in would either lose the guard on Value or impose it on
Promotion where it guards nothing. So the **decision** is shared and each card
keeps its own fallback — one line at each call site.

"Applied" means **reachable**: an unreachable typed target already disables
Apply and must not freeze the sliders as well.

### A hazard found while wiring

`handleSliderChange` was declared **above** the target state it now reads. A
`useCallback` dependency array is evaluated during render, so a callback defined
above its dependencies throws before it is ever called. It was moved below them,
with a comment left at the old site saying why. The full read-set is listed —
six entries on the Value card — which is D3-04's lesson applied rather than
re-learned.

### One new locale key

or below the reachable ARPU range, with `bound` an ARPU. The wall is a different
claim — the slider stopped — so reusing them would have said something false.

## Limits of this check

- **No mounted 4-member case**, as above. The minimum-change branch is
  exercised only by the engine spec; every mounted drag here is three-band and
  therefore exactly determined.
- **The brute comparison sweeps at 0.05 and allows 1e-3 slack.** It shows the
  solved point is not beaten by a coarse search; it is not a proof of
  optimality.
- **The active-set loop is not exercised at a corner in the mounted cases.**
  Nothing here drives a drag whose least-squares answer goes negative and has to
  be pinned; the engine checks reach it only incidentally.
- **The wall's `member` field is computed but never rendered.** The copy names
  the share, not the tier that formed the wall, so a wrong `member` would not
  fail any check.
- **One fixture, one segment.** The mounted numbers are the trimmed fixture's
  three Product L2 tiers under Corporate, and the reachable interval is that
  fixture's.
- **Trap 146 reddens one check.** If the wall message were removed as well, the
