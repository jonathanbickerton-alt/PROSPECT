### Is the promotion applied at All, or dropped?

A zero cannot tell those apart, so the rate was amplified and the same KPI
re-read:

```
inflow promotion     arpu x1  0.00   x10  0.00   x100  0.08   x1000  0.82
retention promotion  arpu x1  0.00   x10  0.01   x100  0.14   x1000  1.45
```

**Linear in the rate** — which is exactly what a pool contribution is. The
pools are applied and reach Base at All. At the real €20.49 the contribution is
**~0.001**, correct for 48 subscribers at €20.49 against a ~340K base, and
below what two decimals can show.

**Jon's arithmetic in the brief is right, and it is the proof that the
promotion is innocent**: 48 subscribers cannot move a 340K base's ARPU by 0.28,
and measurement confirms they move it by 0.001.

### The mechanism, stated

**The sign flip is the yield event.** It re-prices every acquired subscriber
through a mix whose Low Value band carries 53.5% share at an overridden ARPU of
10, and contributes **−0.27** to Base ARPU on its own — enough to carry the sum
past zero from the absolute event's +0.14.

### One real but separate limit, found while measuring

`WhatIfTab.tsx:952` and `:956` round **both** `<scenario> ARPU (Baseline)` and
`(Adjusted)` to two decimals, and `impactSummary` subtracts the two **already
rounded** figures (`:4335-4337`). The displayed delta therefore carries up to
±0.01 of pure rounding and cannot express a movement smaller than that. It
bounds what this KPI can say. It is **not** the cause of a 0.28 swing, and it
was not changed.

## Item 2 — NOT BUILT, and that is the finding

The brief's Item 2 says "fix the mechanism found". **The mechanism found is
that the promotion path is correct**, so there is nothing in it to fix; the
movement belongs to the yield event.

Building a "fix" here would have meant inventing a defect to repair. The
STOP-AND-MEASURE clause exists for the case where the numbers do not show a
mechanism; this is the adjacent case, where they show one and it exonerates the
suspect.

**Whether −0.27 is correct for that yield event is not settled here.** It is
plausible on its face — the mix re-prices all inflow and the largest band is
overridden down to 10 — but plausible is not measured, and it is a different
question from the one this session was given. Recorded in `EXPECTED.md` for
Jon rather than answered unasked.

## Item 3 — D5-01, the unit

### Measured first: two correct formatters already existed, and two did not

| site | percentage renders | correct |
|---|---|---|
| `volumeEventSummary` (`forecasting.ts:769-771`) | `+10%` | **yes** |
| the Volume tab's `fmtDelta` (`WhatIfTab.tsx:5916`) | `+10.0%` | **yes** |
| `promoEventSummary` (`forecasting.ts:783`) | `10` | **no** |
| the promotions table's Volume cell (`WhatIfTab.tsx:7722`) | `+10` | **no** |

The Volume tab's own comment already states the rule this session is applying:
*"'+10' for a 10% uplift reads as ten people. The unit is what distinguishes
them."*

### The shared rule owns the percentage half only

```ts
export function eventVolumeLabel(
  e: { amountType?: 'absolute' | 'percentage'; subscriberVolume: number },
  fmtAbsolute: (n: number) => string,
): string {
  if (e.amountType !== 'percentage') return fmtAbsolute(e.subscriberVolume);
  const sign = e.subscriberVolume > 0 ? '+' : '';
  return `${sign}${e.subscriberVolume}%`;
}
```

**Absolute formatting stays with each caller, deliberately.** The table rounds,
the summary uses `toLocaleString`, the Volume tab uses the injected
`formatNumber` — three real, pre-existing differences. What must not differ
between surfaces is the percentage form, so that is the only half shared. Three
callers: `volumeEventSummary`, `promoEventSummary`, the table cell.

**Absolute output is unchanged at every surface**, asserted rather than
asserted-of: `Promotion 8,000` keeps its lack of a sign, `Inflow +8,000` keeps
its sign, and the table keeps its `+` and its rounding.

**The Volume tab's `fmtDelta` was NOT folded in** — it renders `+10.0%`, one
decimal, and is pinned by existing checks. Unifying it would change a shipped
display to satisfy a tidiness argument. Reported as a remaining duplication,
not silently resolved.

**Zero locale changes.** The sign and the `%` are punctuation, not words; no
key's text moved.
