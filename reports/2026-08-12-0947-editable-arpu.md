## 1. Request 2 (the Value card) — HELD, and why

The brief said to hold and present any settled semantic that proves ambiguous
rather than choose. This one is ambiguous in the design record itself.

The record's rule is: *"Service Revenue becomes a DERIVED per-view display and
stops being an independent input. Two independently editable boxes for one
relationship is how they drift apart."* On the Value card, the read-only
calculated figure is **New blended ARPU** — `Σ share × tierBaseArpu`, which
*follows from the mix the user is setting*. Two readings, materially different:

| reading | consequence |
|---|---|
| **(a)** the blended ARPU becomes editable | creates exactly the two-boxes-one-relationship the record forbids. It is also, functionally, "type a target and let the mix conform" — the constrained mix mode, whose extension is on hold |
| **(b)** the per-tier **Base ARPU** column becomes editable | consistent with the record; the blend stays derived |

**A finding that should inform whichever is chosen.** The Value card's "vs
baseline" delta compares a share-weighted blend against an **equal-weight
mean** — `baselineBlendedArpu` is `Σ tierArpu / n`, its own comment says
"Approximate", and its label says so. Making the weighted side editable while
the comparator stays an unweighted mean would compound a comparison that is
already between two different things.

## 2. Request 1 — built, and a correction to its own premise

The record says a percentage event "today calculates a figure and shows it
read-only". **It showed nothing.** The ARPU and Revenue cells render only when
the draft is absolute. Meanwhile the stored event *did* carry a rate —
`resolveEventArpuRevenue` auto-fills the target cohort's trailing average
whenever ARPU is blank on an Inflow or Retention event. So the figure existed,
applied to the forecast, and was invisible and uneditable. The request was
better founded than its own wording.

### The absence carrier, and why `arpu` could not be it

Three states must stay distinct: **unset** (use the derived rate), **stated**,
and **stated as zero** (a free acquisition).

- `arpu` cannot hold them: the auto-fill makes "user typed 30" and "app filled
  30" identical.
- Truthiness cannot hold them: a stated `0` is falsy.

So known-ness gets its own carrier — the presence of `arpuOverride`. Every read
tests `!== undefined`. The stored carrier is `''`, exactly as
`Promo_Pricing_Amount` uses it, and **`readOptionalNumber` is now the one
definition of that carrier for both fields**. `Number('')` is `0`, so the
emptiness test must precede the numeric one or every blank cell reads as a
deliberate zero.

`resolveEventArpuRevenue` was deliberately **not** extended: its `if (rawArpu)`
is right for callers who mean "blank" and wrong for an override. `draftEventRate`
sits ahead of it and tests for presence.

## 3. The defect the gate found — five writers, not four

I claimed four construction sites. There were **five**. The fifth is
`App.tsx`'s `addMarketEvent`, and it is the **default** add path:
`handleAddMarketEvent` routes to it whenever month-spreading is off, which it is
unless the user turns it on. It built its rate inline and never carried
`arpuOverride`.

**So an ordinary Add click silently discarded the rate the user had typed**, and
the input gave no hint of it.

This is the promo-field defect — a field added to some writers and not all — one
session after the session that existed to close it. It survived the round-trip
spec, the mounted card spec and two guard-traps, because **all of them drive a
hand-built event** and none can reach the real save path: the mounted harness
passes a noop `addMarketEvent`. It was found by stage 2 counting the writers
instead of believing my count.

Three things changed:

1. `draftEventRate` moved to `forecasting.ts` so App can share it. All five
   sites route through it.
2. It owns the **percentage rule** too. Two sites had inline
   `isPct ? { arpu: 0, revenue: 0 } : …` — correct before the rate was editable,
   and a silent zeroing of a stated rate after. Verified a no-op for the
   no-override case.
3. **The lasting guard is a count, not a claim.** `spec:override-arpu` asserts
   five `draftEventRate` call sites and five sites persisting the field, and
   **guard-trap 61** removes it from App's literal to prove that check bites. A
   source check nobody can plant against is decoration.

## WALK INSTRUCTIONS — the three things your eyes gate

Step 2 → **Volume** card → switch the amount toggle from **Subs** to **%**.

**1. The pre-population, and that it says where it came from.** A box labelled
"ARPU for these subscribers" appears, empty, showing a greyed number as its
placeholder. Underneath it should say that number is the **target cohort's**
trailing 3-month average. The thing to check is that the caption names the
source at all — a pre-filled box that does not say it is a default reads as a
number somebody chose. Note it is the *target* cohort's, not whatever your
filter is pointing at; change the event's segment and it should move, change
your view filter and it should not.

**2. Edited vs default, distinguishable at a glance.** Type a rate. The box
should change appearance and the caption should switch to claiming it as yours,
with a "Use the default" link back. Then **clear the box** and confirm it
returns to the default state rather than sitting at zero. Then type **0** — that
must read as *your rate*, not as an empty box. Zero is a real answer (a free
acquisition) and the whole carrier exists to keep it apart from "unset".

**3. Reload survival.** Type a rate, click **Add Event**, export the session,
reload it, and reopen that event. The rate must come back exactly as you typed
it. **Please do this with spreading OFF** — that is the path that was broken
until this session's gate caught it, and it is the one an ordinary click takes.
Worth also checking the events table's ARPU column now shows your rate on a
percentage row rather than a dash.
