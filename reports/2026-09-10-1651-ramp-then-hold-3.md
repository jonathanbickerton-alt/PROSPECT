## 2. The lag — measured, not changed

**Jon's 6,000 is correct, and it is not the last month's own row counted.**

Measured through the 10 Sep 08:43 save, resolving the aggregate with the app's
own two exported seams — `buildRestoredLeafIndex` then `resolveFromStore` —
because the store holds **leaves** and a fit under an All-bearing key is
ignored by design. Nothing was summed by hand.

```
resolved? true | leaves 15 | months 24  2026-07 -> 2028-06
seed 0 known false
campaign start 2026-09 = index 2  ->  22 rows, not 23
rows 100 / 200 / 300, then 300 x 19      sum ALL = 6,300
```

| month | base delta |
|---|---|
| 2026-09 (row 100) | **0.00** |
| 2026-10 (row 200) | 100.00 |
| 2026-11 (row 300) | 300.00 |
| 2026-12 (row 300) | 600.00 |
| … | … |
| **2028-05** | **5,700.00** |
| **2028-06** (last) | **6,000.00** |

**The T+1 rule holds exactly.** The base delta at month *k* is the sum of the
rows in months 1..*k*−1: the campaign's own first month reads **0.00**, and the
last month reads 6,300 − 300 = **6,000**, which is every row strictly before
it. `100 + 200 + 300 × 19` is not "the last row counted" — it *is* the
sum-excluding-the-last, because the campaign has **22** rows and not 23.

**5,700 is the delta at May 2028**, one month earlier. The expectation of 5,700
at June 2028 assumes a 21-row campaign; the horizon from Sep 2026 to Jun 2028
inclusive is 22 months.

**Retention and Inflow are identical** — same row list, same deltas, same
month-by-month arrival. Both reach Base through the same lag and neither
touches it in its own month.

**Where the lag is applied**, `WhatIfTab.tsx`:

```ts
const newBBase = Math.max(0, p_bBase + p_prevBBaseIn - p_prevBBaseOut);   // :1581
...
p_prevBBaseIn  = m.baseline.inflow;                                        // :2302
p_prevBBaseOut = m.baseline.outflow;
p_bBase = newBBase;
```

The month's base is rolled from the **previous** month's inflow and outflow,
and those are assigned at the *end* of the loop body — so a row landing in
month *k* cannot reach Base before month *k*+1. That is the whole mechanism,
and it is one line.

**Nothing was changed.** Session 1's mounted case (b) measured
`100 + 200 + 300 × 21 = 6,600` on a 24-row campaign and called it "sum of rows
strictly before the last"; this measurement says the same rule on a 22-row one.
The two agree.

*Caveat stated:* the resolved aggregate reports `seedBaseKnown false` and
`seedBaseVolume 0`, so the Base series here is a pure delta over an unknown
stock. That does not affect the *delta* figures above — they are differences
of two runs over the same seed — but it means the absolute Base level on this
cohort is not a stock reading.

## 3. One row per month, not two

The brief says the Promotion card emits "2 rows per month". **It emits one.**
`buildPromoEvents` returns a single `MarketEvent` per month carrying the
volume, the mix, the pricing arm and the ARPU together, and all three callers
(add, row edit, campaign save) go through it.

Asserted rather than argued, in `spec:promo-hold-mounted` case 1: 24 rows
across 24 distinct months, one each. I built to what the code does and flagged
the discrepancy rather than inventing a second row.

## 4. A percentage promo campaign has no pill

The briefed reopen case is **+10% Inflow**, and it cannot be exercised: D5-05's
bar refuses a percentage campaign, which the harness read out of the Volume
table verbatim —

> *"Percentage events are edited individually, not as a campaign spread"*

— because campaign group edit reverse-engineers a ramp by summing
`Math.abs(subscriberVolume)`, which is meaningless on a row storing a percent.
That bar predates this session and is not its to change.

So case 4 exercises the restore with an **absolute** promotion — 3,000 over a
3-month ramp under hold, i.e. 1,000 / 2,000 / 3,000 then 3,000 held — and the
finding stands: **a held percentage promo campaign cannot be reopened as a
campaign.** Reported, not repaired.
