## 3. Item 1 — the row-source seam

Re-checked against the source first. Two counts in the brief and the inventory
do not match the code: `applyImportSaveWorkbook` had **11**
`sheet_to_json` calls (not 13), and `exportSession` builds **11** sheets (not
12): Actuals, Baseline_Forecasts, Market_Events, Adjusted_Forecasts,
Bulk_Generation_History, Model_Acceptance_Log, Yield_Events, Pricing_Events,
One_Off_Months, Tariff_Selection, Metadata. The work used the measured figures.

**Where the functions live, and why.** The restore, the save record and the
baseline hand-off are now **module-scope exported functions in `src/App.tsx`**,
with each setter passed in through a `sink` and destructured under its own
name. That way the bodies are the text that stood in App, apart from the sheet
reads. Two reasons:
- a closure inside the component cannot be driven by a spec, and the brief's
  comparisons need to call these functions;
- six traps (15, 51, 82, 83, 218, 292) and five specs anchor on this exact text
  in `src/App.tsx`. A move to a utils file would have re-targeted all of them;
  keeping it in place re-aimed four specs and no traps.

`readProvenance` (pure) moved to module scope with it, because the restore reads
it.

| Part | What it is now |

## 5. Item 3 — the forecast settings (clause 22)

**Where they live** (grep, at the src commit):
- `src/App.tsx:970`: `const [preHorizonUncertainty, setPreHorizonUncertainty] = useState(1.0);`
- `src/App.tsx:972`: `const [postHorizonExpansionRate, setPostHorizonExpansionRate] = useState(1.0);`
- `src/App.tsx:973`: `const [confidenceHorizon, setConfidenceHorizon] = useState(3);`
- `src/App.tsx:963`: `const [stdForecastLength] = useState(24);`. **There is no setter.**

These lines are at the src commit; before this session they were
:175 / :177 / :178 / :168. The module-scope functions inserted above the
component moved them.

The setters are written by StandardForecastTab's controls:
- `setPreHorizonUncertainty` at :923 and :928;
- `setPostHorizonExpansionRate` at :945 and :950;
- `setConfidenceHorizon` at :963;
- all three again by the recommendation "apply" at :887-889.

There is no forecast-length control anywhere in Step 1.

**Built.** The restore reads `Pre_Horizon_Uncertainty_Pct`,
`Post_Horizon_Expansion_Rate_Pct` and `Confidence_Horizon_Months` from Metadata,
and sets each only when it is a finite number. That means a number cell, or a
non-blank string that parses as one. Absent, blank, or non-numeric leaves the
current value. It runs after the rest of the restore succeeds, and refits
nothing.

**HELD: `Forecast_Length_Months`.** With no setter and no control, restoring 18
would change what the next generation does, with nothing on screen showing it
and no way back short of a reload. That is a product decision, not a build
detail. Whether to add a setter and a Step 1 control, or to keep 24 fixed and
leave clause 22 at three settings, is **for Jon**. spec:row-source pins the
held state (it is not read, and `stdForecastLength` has no setter), so lifting
the hold is a deliberate act.

**Proved mounted.** spec:row-source mounts **App itself** and drops the session
on the real Import Save input. It then reads the Step 1 controls through three
new test ids on StandardForecastTab's inputs. No user-facing string was added.
- **PRESENT:** session P (7 / 4 / 5 / 18, Active_Step standard) over the
  defaults: Step 1 shows **7 / 4 / 5**.
- **CONTROL:** session Q (Metadata without the four fields, and no active
  cohort recorded, so its restore raises the fallback notice) over the
  defaults: Step 1 shows **1 / 1 / 3**. This shows that Q carries nothing.
- **ABSENT:** session H (7 / 4 / 5 / 18, stays on Home), then Q over it. Step 1
  shows **7 / 4 / 5**, and the fallback notice proves Q's restore ran.
- **Bulk_Generation_History is unaffected.** Its run keeps 1.5 / 2.25 / 2 / 12
  while the session's own settings restore as 7 / 4 / 5.
- **Not a finite number:** blank, `'wide'` and absent cells set nothing, and
  `'4'` sets 4. **For Jon:** counting a numeric string as finite was my reading
  of "finite number". BigQuery may return Metadata values as STRING.
