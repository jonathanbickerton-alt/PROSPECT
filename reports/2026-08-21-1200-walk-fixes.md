## 1. Fix 1 — the diagnosis, and why nothing was built

### The excluding term, named

The brief asked for it read-only and without assuming the mechanism. It is at
`WhatIfTab.tsx:512`, in the group builder:

```ts
const anyChurn = g.rows.some(e => e.churnMode === 'churn');
if (anyChurn) {
  g.editable = false;
  g.reason = 'Churn-targeted events are edited individually — a campaign spread cannot restate their rates';
} else if (anyPercentage) {
```

**It is an explicit, dated, reasoned bar** — not a predicate keying on
spread-group membership or on a volume field churn rows lack. The brief's
history-rhymes hypothesis (one flag, two jobs, as with the dead churn Add
branch) was the right thing to check and is **wrong here**: this term exists for
churn and nothing else, and it is ordered *before* `anyPercentage` so that its
reason is the one the user sees.

### The premise is refuted on both limbs

**(i) D5 says the opposite of what the brief reads it as saying.** EXPECTED.md,
decision 5 of 2026-08-20, verbatim:

> **5. Churn rows join the group-edit BARRED CLASS** at the `:512` rule, beside
> percentage rows. Campaign group-edit reverse-engineers a ramp by summing
> `|subscriberVolume|`; on a churn row that arithmetic SUCCEEDS and is wrong,
> producing a plausible spread that would not reproduce the stated rates.

The brief renders this as *"churn campaigns JOIN the :512 bar"* and reads
"join the bar" as *gaining the group-edit affordance*. **The `:512` bar is a
prohibition, not a toolbar.** Joining it means being refused.

The walked observation — the brush renders beside a standard campaign and not
beside a churn ramp — is therefore **the specified behaviour, working**.

**(ii) The decline reason does not point at a door that does not exist.** It
reads, in six locales:

> *"This month is one step of a churn ramp — its target is cumulative, so it
> cannot be edited on its own. **Remove the campaign and restate it.**"*

That is precisely the route decision 2 specifies: *"The whole campaign is
recreated instead."* It names recreation, not group edit. There is no dangling
door.

### What building it would have cost

Not merely a reversal of opinion — a **deletion of live guards**:

- `spec:churn-fold` asserts the `anyChurn` term exists, that it is ordered
  before `anyPercentage`, and that it sets `editable = false` with a reason.
- **guard-trap 97**, *"churn rows leave the group-edit barred class"*, plants
  exactly the removal Fix 1 asks for, and currently CATCHES.

So the brief's trap (a) — *"restore the excluding predicate term → the
campaign-bar spec red"* — **already exists**, aimed the other way. Implementing
Fix 1 would mean deleting a passing spec and a passing trap in order to
reintroduce the defect they were written to prevent: a plausible, wrong ramp
spread reverse-engineered from summed volumes, with nothing about it to look
wrong.

**Per the reserved-decisions rule, this conflict is reported rather than
resolved here.** If D5 is to change, that is your call and it should be a brief
of its own, because it needs the re-state semantics designed — the plural
`excludeId` the brief sketches is real work, not a flag flip.

## 2. Fix 2 — the churn Add was enabled but inert

**The defect, exactly as walked.** The Add button's predicate was:

```ts
disabled={!newEvent.date || newEvent.subscriberVolume === undefined}
```

**Churn-blind.** Meanwhile `churnBlockReason` — which already existed, already
carried the seam's own sentence, and was already rendered inside the panel — was
read by the *handler* (`if (churnBlockReason) return`) and by nothing else. So
on a draft whose slice has no baseline the button stayed live, the click reached
the handler, and the handler silently discarded it.

**The fix is the pricing card's shipped shape, exactly**: one predicate, read by
the button, by the message beside it, and by the handler's guard. The button is
disabled, and the reason renders beside it — the same sentence the panel shows,
asserted equal, because two different sentences for one state teaches a user to
distrust both.

**The pin stands at two.** `churnBlockReason` reads the **existing**
`churnScopeResolution`, which has been caller 1 of `resolveEventScopeForecast`
since the 1050 session. No new call site, so no re-pin and no trap re-baseline —
verified by count, not assumed.

## 3. Fix 3 — the tooltip named the metric, not the campaign

A campaign's member rows carry `campaignName` and an **empty** `name` — the
emitter sets `name: newEvent.name || ''`. The tooltip read:

```tsx
{e.name || e.scenario}
```

so a ramp saved with only a campaign name fell straight through to `e.scenario`
and **every month of it read "Outflow"** — three identical rows naming the
metric instead of the campaign that produced them. Now
`{e.name || e.campaignName || e.scenario}`; a per-row name still wins where one
was typed, being the more specific of the two.

**No locale string was involved** — the change is which field is read, not what
text is printed. The shed order's first item (the tooltip locale sweep) was
therefore moot rather than shed.

## Limits of this check

**The mount is jsdom.** State, handlers and conditional rendering under real
props; not layout, not real event ordering, not CSS.

**The tooltip is checked at SOURCE, not rendered.** It is a render function
passed to the chart rather than mounted markup, so the assertion is a text
match on the expression. It proves the expression is right; it does not prove
the tooltip draws.

**Fix 2 is exercised on one absence.** The unresolvable-slice path is driven;
the other `churnBlockReason` branches (no month, no target, the three fold
absences) share the predicate and the button now reads it, but each is not
individually mounted.

**Fix 1's diagnosis is a source and record reading**, not a driven check. I did
not mount a churn campaign to watch the bar refuse it — the existing
`spec:churn-fold` assertions and trap 97 already cover that, and the disputed
question was what the decision says, not what the code does.
