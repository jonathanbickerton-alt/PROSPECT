an assertion in the spec, not a new anchor in the registry* — was right about

## Limits of this check

- **The 87 sites are a TEXTUAL count, not a proof of danger.** `X[0].` after a
  check that guarantees length is perfectly safe; the number is an upper bound
  on candidates, not a defect count.
- **Nothing was added that would FAIL if a new unguarded dereference appeared.**
  The count is recorded in this report only. A baselined `spec:survival` that
  asserts the per-file counts exactly, in both directions, is the obvious next
  step and was not taken here.
- **The two guarded specs are green on the unmutated tree and red under their
  own trap. Neither was re-run under any OTHER trap's mutation**, so a second
  death path in either would still be unknown.
- **Item 4's 42/15 split is derived from the registry's `spec:` fields**, read
  statically. A trap that switched spec would move the boundary without anyone
  re-running this count.
- **`churn-fold-spec` at 27 sites was not opened.** Its count is high enough
  that it deserves its own look, which this session did not give it.
