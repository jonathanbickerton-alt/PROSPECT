- **guard-traps was not run.** The 61 specs are green on `d7e863c`; the 177
  traps were last proven at `d63e67a`. No source has changed since `78e06de`.
- **The document was not read end to end for sense.** Splice boundaries were
  spot-checked at six points and the structure verified by heading list; the
  claim made here is provenance and integrity, not editorial coherence.
