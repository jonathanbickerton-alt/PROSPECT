## Item 2 — D5-05

**No new key.** The reason already existed in six locales; what was built is
that it is **rendered as text** beside the pill, on both cards, with
`aria-disabled="true"` on the pill itself.

`aria-disabled`, not `aria-pressed`: the element is a `<span>`, not a toggle,
and has no pressed state to report. Disabled, **not hidden** — a user who can
see the campaign is told why it cannot be opened.

```

  reason "Percentage events are edited individually, not as a campaign spread"
pct campaign PROMO   row pencils 2
  reason "Percentage events are edited individually, not as a campaign spread"
```

Both cards, the same sentence, and the Promotion card still offers **one pencil
per row** — which is the whole of what the bar leaves open.

twice rather than extracting a component: an extraction mid-session would be a
third refactor on a file already carrying three inline amount-mode controls.
Recorded as duplication rather than silently doubled.
